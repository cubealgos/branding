# Mascot: the clownfish

Decision 13: the secondary character is a chubby clownfish with a forked tail. It is called "the clownfish"
(or "the fish"). All files are brand assets: all rights reserved, see `assets/LICENSE.md`.

## Silhouette rules

- **One flat silhouette** in every pose: a body with all four corners cut (the logo's language,
  `M54 8 H90 L110 28 V52 L90 72 H54 L36 54 V26 Z`), a forked triangle tail (`M60 40 L14 14 L27 40 L14 66 Z`) and
  one vertical band (x 60, 12 wide) clipped to body and tail. Two ink bar eyes (7 x 16 at x 82 and 95, y 28) in the
  default pose.
- **No outlines and no drawn details.** The silhouette alone says "fish" and "cute"; nothing is drawn on top of a shape
  except the eyes. Body and tail never carry a stroke; the only strokes are on the happy `^ ^` eyes and the dead `x x`
  eyes (square caps).
- Only the eyes, the posture (tilt, flip) and small extras outside the shape change between poses (the `?`, the `z Z`)
  plus the tear.
- **Two colour versions only**, from the colour tokens:

| Version | Body | Band | Eyes | For |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| standard (`assets/fish/`) | amber `#D9831A` | `fish-band` `#E7CEB0` (1.9:1 on the body, decoration only) | ink `#16181D` | every background except amber |
| on amber (`assets/fish/on-amber/`) | white `#FBFBFC` | `#E7CEB0` (now darker than the body) | ink | amber backgrounds |

The extras (`?`, `z Z`) are ink by default and read `--fish-extra`, so inline SVG on an ink background sets
`--fish-extra: #EDEEF1` (paper); a standalone `<img>` on ink shows them in ink, so use the exports in #12 or inline it.
The tear is always the band colour.

## Poses and the states they mark

| Pose | Key frame | State |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `idle` | bar eyes open | default |
| `swimming` | bar eyes, level | loading |
| `happy` | `^ ^` eyes (`M82 38 L85.5 30 L89 38 M95 38 L98.5 30 L102 38`, stroke 3.4) | sent, saved |
| `confused` | uneven eyes, tilted -8 degrees, a `?` beside the fish | 404 |
| `sad` | drooping slanted eyes, tilted 9 degrees (nose down), a tear in the band colour | errors that are not a 404 |
| `asleep` | closed eyes, tilted 4 degrees, a rising blocky `z Z` | empty states |
| `dead` | belly-up (flipped), `x x` eyes, tilted -14 degrees, no water line | crashes and fatal errors |

## Files and generation

`assets/fish/<pose>.svg` and `assets/fish/on-amber/<pose>.svg` for the seven poses (14 files), each a self-contained SVG
(`viewBox="-4 -6 144 90"`, which holds every extra and tilt without clipping; `role="img"` and an `aria-label` such as
"Idle clownfish"; no external reference). Everything is generated by `scripts/fishkit.mjs` (the geometry, ported from the
prototype's `fishkit.py`) and `scripts/generate-fish.mjs`: `npm run build:fish` rewrites the files, colours are read from
`dist/json/tokens.json`, and `npm run check:fresh` fails if a committed file differs. `test/fish.test.mjs` diffs the
silhouette, tail and band across all poses and checks the stroke, colour, accessibility and viewBox rules.
